# 📣 What's new in 0.29.0

Smaller fixes and tidy-ups that make running content easier.

## 🛠️ Fixed
**Adding a slot no longer moves your players.** Before, adding a line in the middle of the slot list shifted the roles and weapons of the players below it. Now a new line just adds a new empty position, and everyone keeps their own position. The numbers can still change, because positions are always ordered Tank, DTank, Healer, Support, DPS.

## ✨ New
**The Manage panel updates by itself.** Press **Lock** and the button turns into **Unlock** right there. The same goes for **End** and **Reopen**. You no longer have to dismiss the message and open Manage again.
*How to use it:* press **Manage** on the roster, then press any of those buttons.

**Two new duties: Party Healer 💚 and Main Healer 💖.**
*How to use it:* Manage → **Set duty**, or type it in the slot list as `Healer - Holy Staff (Main Healer)`.

**New categories.**
• PvE: Static dungeon, Dragon sanctuary, Hellgate, Depths, Avalonian raid, Crystal creatures and Ancient Lands.
• PvP: Outposts, Castle and Ancient Lands.
• Fame farming and Avalonian gold chest are retired. Content that already uses them keeps its category.
• PvX leaves out Training, so its menu fits Discord's 25-option limit.

**A clearer calendar.** Event titles are bigger and have no emoji, and the details below them are now normal-size text.
