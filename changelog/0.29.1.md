# 📣 What's new in 0.29.1

A small follow-up to 0.29.0.

**Mists and Corrupted dungeon are now PvP categories.** They were listed under PvE. Content you already made keeps its category.

**Category menus are sorted A to Z**, with Other always last. This includes the PvX menu.

**New PvE category: Ava Skip 🛤️.**

**PvX** now leaves out Skirmish as well as Training, so its menu still fits Discord's 25-option limit. Content already saved with either one keeps its label.
