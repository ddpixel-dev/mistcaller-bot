# 📣 What's new in 0.30.3

## 📌 Find the roster fast
The roster scrolls away as people talk. Now it is easy to get back to it:
• **The roster is pinned** in its post. Tap the 📌 icon at the top of the post to open it.
• **The bot's posts have a 📋 Roster button**: when a fill is seated, a player is accepted or declined, someone is removed, or a position opens for you. One tap takes you to the roster.

*Admins:* give the bot the **Pin Messages** permission in each event forum, so it can pin. Without it everything still works, only the pin is skipped.

## 🔁 Fills and selection events
**Fill is simpler.** The Fill panel offers **Any position** and **Any [role] position**. You can still pick several roles.

**Hosts only see positions that fit.** When you place a fill or accept an applicant, the position menu lists only the open positions that suit what they asked for. If none fits, the panel says so.

**A tidier selection roster.** There is a little space between the players and the applicants, and the note is shorter: *"Selection event: the host picks the party."*

## 🗂️ Categories
**Avalonian raid is removed** from the PvE categories (older events keep their label). **Skirmish** is back in the PvX menu.

## 📢 For admins
**The changelog can go in a forum.** With `/content setup` → `changelog-channel` you can now pick a forum: each release becomes its own post there (the bot needs Create Posts and Send Messages in Posts). A text or announcement channel works as before.
