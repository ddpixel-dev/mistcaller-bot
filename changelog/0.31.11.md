# 🛠️ What's new in 0.31.11

## ✅ Hosts can always place an applicant
Fixed: when two players applied for the same position and the host accepted one, the other could not be placed anywhere.
- **Hosts:** in **Review applicants** and **Add player**, the position menu now lists every open position. The ones the player asked for come first, marked **Asked for**. Taken positions are never listed.
- An applicant whose position was filled shows **(taken)**, so you know to pick another position for them.

## ✏️ Edit a preset
- **Admins:** on the creation card, press **Manage presets → Edit preset** and pick a preset. It loads into the card. Change what you need, then press **Manage presets → Save preset**. You can rename it in the same form.

## 🗂️ Categories
- **PvE:** new **Tracking**.
- **PvP:** **Skirmish** is gone.
- **ZvZ:** new **Territory Attack**. **Outpost** and **Castle** are now one option, **Outpost & Castle**.
- Events posted before keep their category.

## 📅 Calendar
- A line now separates the **Call to arms** events from the other content of a day.
- Another line separates **Live now** from the upcoming days.

## ✨ Roster and buttons
- The roster's **Manage** button is now **Edit content**. Everything inside it is the same.
- **Assign player** is now **Add player**, and **Remove player** has a ❌ icon.
- Party titles use 👥, so they no longer look like the Tank and DPS icons.
- In events where the host picks the party, the note is now a small line under **Your time**.
