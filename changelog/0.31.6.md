# 📣 What's new in 0.31.6

## ❓ Tentative
Not sure you can make it? Sign up as usual, then press **Tentative** next to Leave. A ❓ appears beside your name, so the host knows to plan for a gap. You keep your place and still count in the party. Press **Tentative** again when you are sure.
It works wherever you are listed: in a position, as a fill, as an applicant, or on the waitlist. If you move or get seated, the ❓ stays. If you leave, it goes. Hosts see the ❓ in Review applicants and Assign player too.
The buttons are now on two rows, with **Manage** on the second.

## 🗓️ Posts show the date and time
Event posts are now named like `Ava roam | 12 Nov | 20:00 UTC`, so events with the same title are easy to tell apart. The name follows the title and the start time when the host edits them. Posts made before this change get the new name the next time they are edited.
