# 📣 What's new in 0.30.0

Smarter fills, and events where the host picks the party.

## 🔁 Fill now means "put me where I fit"
Before, choosing **Fill** only meant "any position", and the host had to place you by hand. Now you choose what you can play, and the bot seats you.

*How to use it:*
1. On the roster, open the position menu and choose **Fill (choose what you can play)**. If the roster is full, the waitlist menu has it too.
2. A private panel opens. Pick **Any position**, one or more roles (for example *Any Healer position*), or specific positions. You can mix them.
3. If a matching position is open, you are **seated at once**, and the post says where: *"@you joined as fill (Healer, #7) and was seated in position 3 · Healer - Holy Staff."*
4. If nothing matching is open, you **wait** as a fill. The roster shows what you asked for. As soon as a matching position frees up, you are seated automatically and the post tells you.

Healer positions are filled first. Waiting players and waiting fills are seated in the order they joined.

**What changed:** fills no longer hold a reserved seat. Every open position can be picked, and the hosts no longer have to place fills by hand (they can still move anyone from Manage). The party count is just the players who have a position. Fills made before this update are seated right away where they fit.

## 🎯 Selection events: the host picks the party
Some content needs a hand-picked party. Now the host can turn on **selection**.

*How to turn it on:* when you create the event, use the **Sign-up** menu on the creation card. Later, open Manage, then **Edit**, then the **Sign-up** menu.

*What players see:* the post says *"Selection event: signing up is an application. The host picks the party."* Picking a position, or using **Fill**, becomes an **application**. It holds no seat, several players can apply for the same position, and applicants are listed on the roster. **Leave** withdraws an application.

*What the host does:* open **Manage**, then **Review applicants**. Pick an applicant, then the position, and they are accepted: the post mentions them. To decline, use **Remove player**: the applicant is mentioned with a short note. Switching back to open sign-up needs the applicants reviewed first.

## 🛠️ For admins
Run `scripts/seat-waiting-fills.ts` once after the update if you want existing fills seated straight away (the release does it).
