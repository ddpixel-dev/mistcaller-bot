# 📣 What's new in 0.31.9

## ⚔️ A CTA is one message, right in the CTA channel
A new CTA is now **one message in the CTA channel**, with both parties in it. You can sign up there without opening the thread. The thread starts from the same message, so you see it at the top of the thread too.
- Each party has its **own menu**: pick a position in the party you want. Picking a position in the other party moves you.
- **Fill** and the waitlist still work inside each party.
- **Leave** and **Tentative** are now one button each for the whole CTA, because you can only be in one place.
- CTAs made with 0.31.8 keep working: their first message shows everything, and the second message points to it.

## 💣 Bombers
A CTA can now have a **Bombers** list: players with no set position.
- **Hosts:** in **Details**, type a **Bombers cap** from 1 to 20. Leave it empty for no Bombers. You can change it later in **Manage → Edit → Edit details** (not below the number of bombers already in).
- **Players:** press **💣 Bombers** next to Leave and Tentative. You are in Bombers instead of a position. Pressing a position moves you out of Bombers, and the other way round.
- Hosts can remove bombers with **Remove player → 💣 Bombers** and mark their attendance there. Ping, reminders and the loot vote include bombers.

## ✏️ Easier CTA card
- **Details** now has only the title, start, gear tier, notes and the Bombers cap.
- Positions have their own buttons: **Slots** (one party) or **Party 1 slots** and **Party 2 slots** (two parties).
- The Parties menu now says **How many parties?** with **One Party** and **Two Parties**.
- In **Manage → Edit** the same split: **Edit details**, **Edit party 1 slots**, **Edit party 2 slots**.

## 💾 Presets for CTAs
Presets you save on the CTA card are listed only on the CTA card (with both parties and the Bombers cap). Presets from Plan Content stay on Plan Content. Each kind can hold 25, and a name can be used once in the server. A CTA preset saved before this update that has only one party shows under Plan Content; save it again from the CTA card.

## 🧹 No more "pinned a message" notices
When the bot pins a message, it now removes Discord's "pinned a message" notice. **Admins:** give the bot the **Manage Messages** permission in the CTA channel and in the event forums, or the notice stays.
