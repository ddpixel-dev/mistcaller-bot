# 🗡️ What's new in 0.32.0

## 🗡️ Slots by weapon
Hosts can now write a roster by **weapon** instead of by role.
- **Hosts:** on the creation card, pick **Slots by → Slots by weapon (Weapon only)**. Then write one weapon per line, for example `Hallowfall (Main Healer)`. The roster shows `1. Hallowfall - @Player - Main Healer`.
- **Slots by role (Role - Weapon)** works as before, and is the default.
- It works for every event, CTAs included. Choose it before you type the slots: switching clears them.
- On a weapon roster, **Fill** and the **waitlist** work by weapon. For example, you can wait for **Bow (all taken)**.
- Presets remember which way their slots are written.

## ✅ Only real roles and weapons
- **Roles** are **Tank, DTank, Healer, Support** and **DPS**. Jobs such as Caller or Scout stay duties, in brackets: `Tank - Mace (Caller)`.
- **Weapons** must be names from the game, off-hands included. Capitals and spaces don't matter: `great axe` is saved as **Greataxe**.
- If you mistype, the bot suggests the closest names, for example: *"Grt Axe" is not a weapon. Did you mean Greataxe?*
- Older rosters and presets keep their names. When you edit an older roster, lines you leave unchanged are accepted as they are.
