# 📣 What's new in 0.30.1

Small improvements to fills and selection events.

**Fill is simpler.** The Fill panel now offers **Any position** and **Any [role] position**. The single-position choices are gone. You can still pick several roles.

**Hosts only see positions that fit.** When you place a player who joined as a fill, or accept an applicant, the position menu lists only the open positions that suit what they asked for. If none fits, the panel says so.

**A tidier selection roster.** There is a little space between the players and the applicants, and the selection note is shorter: *"Selection event: the host picks the party."*

**Avalonian raid is removed** from the PvE categories. Older events that used it keep their label. **Skirmish** is back in the PvX menu.
